WHAT WE DO

Solutions built around agriculture.

At Okafor Agriscience, we focus on practical agricultural solutions that address the needs of farmers and modern food production.

Our solutions span crop protection, plant nutrition, seeds and animal health, supported by scientific knowledge and an understanding of farming.

01 — CROP PROTECTION

Protecting crops from the threats that reduce yield.

Healthy crops are the foundation of productive farming. Our crop protection solutions help farmers manage the major threats that affect crop health and yield.

  • Insect and pest management
  • Disease management
  • Weed control
  • Crop protection programmes

We combine product solutions with practical agricultural knowledge to help farmers protect their crops throughout the production cycle.

Nutrition for stronger crop growth.

Plants require the right nutrients, in the right amounts, at the right stages of growth.

Our plant nutrition solutions support crop development, plant health and productivity through fertilizers and targeted micronutrient solutions.

  • Micronutrients
  • Crop nutrition programmes

Health and nutrition for better animal production.
