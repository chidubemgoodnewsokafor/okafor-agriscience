OUR APPROACH

Understanding agriculture through science.

Agriculture is built on science — from the biology of crops and animals to genetics, nutrition, protection and technology.

At Okafor Agriscience, we use scientific knowledge and practical agricultural understanding to identify solutions that can help farmers produce better, and in turn enough food for the growing population.

01 • CROP SCIENCE

Science for healthier, more productive crops.

Crop science helps us understand how plants grow, respond to their environment and produce efficiently.

We consider plant health, crop nutrition, protection, varieties and growing conditions when developing agricultural solutions.
